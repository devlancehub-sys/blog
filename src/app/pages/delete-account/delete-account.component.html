Important: Account deletion is permanent. Your profile, coin balance, watch history, and related data cannot be recovered.

1. Who Can Request Deletion

Any registered TalkyMate user may request account deletion, including:

  • Viewer app users — users who browse videos and purchase coins
  • Creator app users — users who upload or publish AI status videos

2. How to Delete Your Account

1

Email support

Send a request to {{ site.supportEmail }} with subject Account Deletion Request.

2

Include account details

Your registered name, username/phone, app type (Viewer or Creator), and confirmation that deletion should be permanent.

3

Verification

Our team may verify ownership before processing your request.

4

Confirmation

You will receive an email once deletion is complete, usually within 7–15 business days.

3. Before You Delete

  • Use remaining coins or request help if a payment issue occurred
  • Creators: withdraw pending earnings before deletion where applicable
  • Log out from all devices after confirmation

4. What Gets Deleted

  • Profile information and login credentials
  • Coin balance and purchase history linked to your account
  • Watch history and saved preferences
  • Creator uploads and payout account details where applicable

5. What TalkyMate Is Not

TalkyMate is not a dating, chat, or live video-calling service. Deleting your account removes your access to the video status platform only.
