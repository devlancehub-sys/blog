Our Mission

TalkyMate makes it easy to discover short, engaging AI-generated videos and status-style content. Our video-player experience is designed for immersive viewing — not live chatting, not dating, and not real person-to-person video calling.

What TalkyMate Is Not

TalkyMate is not a dating app, chat app, matchmaking service, or live video-calling platform. The player interface may resemble a call screen for visual design purposes, but playback is pre-recorded or streamed video content — not a real-time call with another user.
